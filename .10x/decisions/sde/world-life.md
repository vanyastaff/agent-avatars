# World life — 0.9.0

User correction: themed worlds must have their own social and leisure life, not
the same workshop with renamed furniture. City: barrel fire, barbecue, music,
stoop gatherings. Ocean: swimming and play. Preserve personality and real-event
reactions. Continue at the already approved public register/event/render seam.

Replaced the 13 non-workshop occupation catalogs with life.ts: 52 places and
pastimes, world-specific pixel art, gesture styles, ambient fire/fish/music,
and personality-selected episode routes. Episodes approach, act and close before
moving on; quiet/normal/lively intervals are 20/12/8 seconds. Tool changes do not
restart or choose episodes. Only active, unblocked actors advance episodes.
Shared spots support small gatherings; two sides of each spot reduce overlap.

Actual workKind, tool tokens, parallel results, permissions and retirement stay
owned by host events. A separate Episode owns the visual destination. Titles,
alt text, terminal rows and /avatars retain truthful tool status; non-workshop
foreground labels show names instead of permanent work bubbles. Accessible life
descriptions also replace the obsolete tool-prop descriptions.

Delivered messages, arrivals, errors and completions retain bounded duets, now
with local vocabulary and personality gestures. Tool calls can coexist with a
leisure duet; questions, approvals, turn boundaries and retirement still cancel
it. The workshop retains its tool choreography and busy-recipient rules. Deleted
the old themed job catalog/art and replaced tests specifying that rejected design.

No new API calls, capabilities, model inference, dependencies or content parsing.
Behavior continues to affect pace and expressive movement; quiet/reduced motion
remain respected. Existing auto skin/world mapping is retained.

Manifest, marketplace and source agree on 0.9.0. Local implementation only.
